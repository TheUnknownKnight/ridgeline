Ridgeline

Backcountry Route Hazard Checker

Privacy Policy GitHub Support & Issues

Know what's on the trail before you step onto it.

Ridgeline is a free, open-source, no-login backcountry hazard checker. Search public US Forest Service & National Park trails — or open any GPX, KML, or GeoJSON track — and cross-reference every mile against live federal hazard feeds and modeled underfoot conditions, with offline support on the trail.

Clear Low Moderate High Extreme

8 Live Data Streams

NIFC wildfires, NWS alerts & gridded forecasts, Avalanche.org zones, NOAA river gauges, EPA AirNow PM2.5, NOAA smoke forecasts, and a physical snow/ice/mud trail model.

Offline On-Trail Cockpit

Save corridor topo tiles and your latest briefing before leaving service. Track trail progress, next hazard ahead, remaining climb, local sunset, and SOS coordinates offline.

Private & Open Source

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no server-side user database. Your device fetches public federal data directly and stores saved routes locally.

Support & Bug Reports

Found an inaccurate hazard reading, a broken trail geometry, or a mobile issue? Ridgeline treats false positives and bad readings as high-priority bugs.

  • Report a bug or inaccurate reading: open an issue at github.com/TheUnknownKnight/ridgeline/issues (include the trail name/mile and screenshot if possible).
  • Privacy & backcountry safety: read the full Privacy Policy & Backcountry Safety Notice.
